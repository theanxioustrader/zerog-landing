How to Use Google Antigravity From Your Phone

Short answer: Google Antigravity is desktop-only — there's no official mobile app. To use it from your phone, you pair a mobile companion app to your running desktop session. The companion relays your commands to Antigravity and streams the agent's replies back to your phone in real time, over Wi-Fi or cellular.

Does Google Antigravity have an official mobile app?

No. Antigravity runs on Windows, macOS, and Linux desktops only. Google has not published an iOS or Android version. Any mobile access to Antigravity — including ZeroG — comes from a companion tool that bridges your phone to the desktop session that's already running.

How do I control Antigravity from my phone?

With a companion app like ZeroG, the flow looks like this:

  1. Install the app. Get ZeroG on your iPhone — free on the App Store.
  2. Add the extension to Antigravity. In Antigravity open the Extensions panel, search ZeroG, click Install — the setup wizard pops up and guides you.
  3. Generate a QR code. Open the ZeroG panel inside Antigravity and click Generate Secure Connection.
  4. Scan it from your phone. In the ZeroG app, go to Settings → Pair new device and scan the code.
  5. Command from anywhere. Send a chat message or terminal command from your phone — it runs on your PC, and the reply streams back live.

Can I use Antigravity on iPhone?

Yes — ZeroG is available now on the App Store for iPhone and pairs directly to your desktop Antigravity IDE. Android support is coming soon.

Does it work over cellular, not just Wi-Fi?

Yes. As long as your computer is on with Antigravity running, ZeroG connects from anywhere over Wi-Fi or cellular through an encrypted relay — your code and AI model stay on your own machine the whole time.

What do I need to get started?

  • An iPhone with the ZeroG app installed
  • A Windows, macOS, or Linux computer running Antigravity
  • The ZeroG extension added to Antigravity (Extensions panel → search ZeroG → Install)

Antigravity has to stay open on your computer for the phone to reach your agent — ZeroG is the remote, not a replacement for the desktop IDE.
