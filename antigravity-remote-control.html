Control Your Antigravity AI Agent Remotely

Short answer: Antigravity remote control means sending commands to your desktop AI agent and getting its responses back from another device — usually your phone — while Antigravity keeps running on your computer. A mobile companion app like ZeroG pairs to your session by QR code and relays chat messages and terminal commands both ways over an encrypted connection.

What does "remote control" mean for Antigravity?

Antigravity itself doesn't move anywhere — it stays running on your PC. "Remote control" refers to a second device, connected to that same session, that can send input and receive output. You're not accessing a copy of Antigravity; you're reaching into the one that's already open on your desktop.

How does Antigravity remote control work?

With ZeroG, the mechanism is a paired, encrypted relay:

  1. The ZeroG extension runs inside Antigravity on your computer and generates a QR pairing code.
  2. You scan that code once from the ZeroG app on your phone.
  3. From then on, messages typed on your phone are injected into Antigravity's chat, and terminal commands run on your PC.
  4. The agent's replies and terminal output stream back to your phone in real time.

Nothing about Antigravity itself changes — ZeroG relays input and output, it doesn't modify the IDE or intercept its network traffic.

Can I approve or steer agent actions remotely?

Yes. Agentic IDEs like Antigravity often pause to ask a question or wait for direction mid-task. With remote control, you can answer, redirect, or approve the next step from your phone the moment it's needed, instead of losing time until you're back at your desk.

Is it safe to remote-control Antigravity?

Two things worth checking in any remote-control tool:

  • Where does your code and AI model actually run? With ZeroG, both stay on your own machine — the app only relays messages between your phone and PC, so your code is never stored or processed on a third-party AI service.
  • Does it modify Antigravity? ZeroG connects to your running session and relays input/output rather than patching the app.

What can't I do remotely?

Your computer has to stay on with Antigravity open — remote control reaches a live session, it can't start Antigravity on a machine that's off or asleep. You're also working through chat and terminal from your phone, not Antigravity's full desktop editor interface.
