FEATURES

Everything Antigravity users need

⚡

Native Antigravity integration

Messages inject straight into Antigravity's chat, and AI responses stream back to your phone in real time.

⌨️

Live terminal

Fire off commands on your PC and watch the output stream in — stdout, errors, and exit codes, line by line.

🔒

Private by design

Your AI model and your code stay on your own machine. ZeroG runs nothing on our servers but the encrypted relay that links your phone to your PC.

🌐

Works anywhere

On Wi-Fi or cellular, at home or on the go. As long as your PC is on, your agent is a tap away.

🔗

Direct IDE connection

ZeroG links straight to your running Antigravity session — what you send drops right into the IDE, and the agent's replies come straight back to your phone.

🔄

Always in sync

Auto-reconnect, missed-message recovery, and offline buffering keep you synced even on spotty connections.
