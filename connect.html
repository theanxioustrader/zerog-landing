Set up in 7 easy steps

Prefer reading? Same walkthrough, every click spelled out. You'll need the ZeroG app on your phone and Antigravity installed on your computer.

1

Install ZeroG on your iPhone

Tap Download on the App Store above. It opens the App Store — tap Get to install ZeroG free. Leave the app for now; we'll come back to the phone at step 6.

2

Download the extension file on your computer

Click Add to Antigravity above. A small file called ZeroG-for-Antigravity.vsix lands in your Downloads folder. Don't double-click it — Antigravity will pick it up in the next step.

3

Open the Extensions panel in Antigravity

Open Antigravity and look at the toolbar on the far left edge of the window. Click the icon that looks like four little squares — that's Extensions. (Shortcut: press Ctrl+Shift+X on Windows, Cmd+Shift+X on Mac.) A panel slides open.

4

Install the file you downloaded

At the top of the Extensions panel, click the ··· (three dots) menu → choose Install from VSIX… → your file picker opens. Go to Downloads, pick ZeroG-for-Antigravity.vsix, and click Install. If a blue Reload button appears, click it — Antigravity restarts in a few seconds.

5

Open ZeroG and make your connection code

After the reload, a new ZeroG icon appears in that same left toolbar — click it. The ZeroG Mobile Bridge panel opens. Click the big Generate Secure Connection button. A QR code fills the panel — leave it on screen.

6

Scan it with your phone

Now grab your iPhone: open the ZeroG app → Settings → Pair new device → point the camera at the QR code on your computer screen. It locks on in a second.

7

You're connected

Your phone shows LIVE and the desktop confirms the link. Type a message or a terminal command — it runs on your PC and the reply streams back to your phone. That's it: your AI now travels with you.

Keep your computer awake. ZeroG connects to your live Antigravity session, so your computer needs to be on with Antigravity open for your phone to reach the agent. If your PC goes to sleep, the phone will show offline until it wakes — for long sessions, set your computer to not sleep while plugged in.

Good to know

A few controls and tips to get the most out of ZeroG.

★

Live status at a glance

The LIVE / OFFLINE badge at the top of the app tells you whether your phone can currently reach your PC. Green means you're good to go.

★

Go to Settings → Pair new device and scan a new computer's code anytime. ZeroG Pro lets you keep multiple computers paired.

★

Frequently asked questions

Do I need Antigravity to use ZeroG?

Yes. ZeroG is the mobile companion for the Antigravity AI IDE — it connects directly to your running Antigravity session. Antigravity is desktop-only, and ZeroG is the way to use it from your phone.

Is my data private?

Your AI model and your code stay on your own machine. ZeroG runs nothing on our servers except the encrypted relay that passes messages between your phone and your PC. Your code is never stored or processed on a third-party AI service.

My phone says OFFLINE — how do I fix it?

Check three things: (1) Antigravity is open on your computer with the ZeroG panel running, (2) your computer is on and connected to the internet, and (3) you scanned the most recent QR code. If it's still offline, generate a fresh code in Antigravity and re-scan it from Settings → Pair new device.

The QR code won't scan.

Make sure the whole code is inside the camera frame with good lighting, and that you allowed camera access when ZeroG asked. Try moving a little closer or further from the screen. You can tap "Scan Again" to retry.

Do I have to keep my computer on?

Yes. Your agent runs on your PC, so your computer and Antigravity must be running for your phone to connect. If your PC sleeps or closes Antigravity, the phone will show offline until it's back.

How do I connect a different computer?

Open the ZeroG panel on the new computer, generate its QR code, then on your phone go to Settings → Pair new device and scan it. Pro lets you pair more than one device.

How do I remove the ads?

The free version shows an ad every few messages. Upgrading to Pro removes ads entirely and unlocks unlimited paired devices and priority speed. You can subscribe from the pricing section on the home page.

Which phones are supported?

iOS is available now. Android is coming soon. As long as your computer is on, you can connect from anywhere on Wi-Fi or cellular.
